Spring cleaning in London, Surrey & the M25

The big clean every home deserves.

Every home needs at least one big clean a year. Ours goes above and beyond the weekly routine — into the corners, cupboards and details that daily life skips.

★★★★★5-star rated on Google by our customersRead our Google reviews

A spring clean is everything a regular clean is, plus everything it isn't: inside cupboards, under furniture, behind appliances, skirting boards, door frames, tiles and grout. A full seasonal reset for your home.

All with non-toxic, biodegradable products — so the "freshly cleaned" feeling is actually fresh air, not chemical fumes.

If you’re looking for a “deep clean” of the whole home, this is it.
