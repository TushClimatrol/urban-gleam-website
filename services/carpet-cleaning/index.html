Carpet and upholstery cleaning in London, Surrey & the M25

Carpets and sofas, properly deep-cleaned.

Professional hot-water extraction cleaning for carpets, rugs, sofas and armchairs — lifting stains, odours and years of trodden-in dirt that vacuuming leaves behind.

★★★★★5-star rated on Google by our customersRead our Google reviews

Carpets collect far more than they show. Our hot-water extraction (steam) method injects a cleaning solution deep into the pile and immediately extracts it along with the dirt, allergens and residue — the method most carpet manufacturers recommend. Stains are pre-treated, high-traffic lanes get extra passes, and carpets are usually dry in 2–4 hours.

Child- and pet-safe solutions, no harsh solvents, and we test an inconspicuous patch first on delicate wool or rugs. Upholstery is cleaned with the same care using a hand tool, including cushions and arms.

You may know it as steam cleaning, carpet shampooing or hot-water extraction; sofa cleaning is often called upholstery cleaning.
