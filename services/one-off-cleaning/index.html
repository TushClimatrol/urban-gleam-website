One-off deep cleaning in London, Surrey & the M25

One visit. Total transformation.

A single, intensive deep clean without any ongoing commitment — perfect before guests arrive, after a busy stretch, or whenever home needs a reset.

★★★★★5-star rated on Google by our customersRead our Google reviews

Sometimes you don't need a cleaner every week — you need one brilliant clean, right now. Our one-off deep clean covers your whole home to the same standard as our regular service, with extra time for the areas that need it most.

You set the priorities: tell us what matters — the kitchen before a dinner party, the bathroom before in-laws, the whole flat before a landlord inspection — and we'll build the visit around it.

Some people call it a one-time clean or a one-off deep clean.
