How booking works

  1. Choose your service. Tell us the size of the job: hours for hourly services, bedrooms for fixed-price cleans.
  2. Pick a date and time. Slots are available seven days a week.
  3. Add your details. Your address, how we get in, and any notes — pets, parking, focus areas.
  4. Confirm. Your booking comes straight to us and we confirm your slot by phone or email. You pay after the clean.
