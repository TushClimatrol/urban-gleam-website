We’re hiring

Looking for cleaners? Come join the team.

Urban Gleam is growing across Sunbury-on-Thames, Surrey and South West London, and we need reliable, friendly cleaners who take pride in their work. Part-time or full-time, experienced or keen to learn.

Apply in 60 secondsAsk on WhatsApp

The role

Cleaner — domestic & commercial (part-time or full-time). Regular weekly and fortnightly home cleans, office and communal area cleaning, plus end of tenancy, after builders, oven, carpet and window cleans for those who want them. You work in customers’ homes and workplaces across our area, either on your own once trained or in a pair for the bigger jobs.

We are a small Sunbury-on-Thames company, rated 5.0 on Google by our customers, and the people you’d be cleaning for are mostly families, landlords, letting agents and local businesses who have used us for years. We take on cleaners who care about doing it properly — because that is why those customers keep coming back.

What we offer

Flexible hours — Part-time or full-time. Tell us the mornings, afternoons, evenings or weekends you can do and we build your rota around them.
Work close to home — Our jobs are in Sunbury, Shepperton, Staines, Walton, Kingston, Twickenham, Richmond and across SW London — we match you to your area.
Regular customers — Weekly and fortnightly homes mean the same addresses and friendly faces, not a different stranger every day.
Everything provided — Eco-friendly, non-toxic products, HEPA vacuums and equipment are supplied, so you never pay for kit.
Paid training — Full training on our methods, the end of tenancy checklist and safe product use — experienced or not.
A small, friendly team — You deal with the owners directly. Good work is noticed and there is room to take on more hours or lead jobs.

What we’re looking for

Reliable and punctual — our customers book slots and count on us turning up
Takes pride in a proper, thorough clean and an eye for detail
Friendly, polite and trustworthy in people’s homes and workplaces
Right to work in the UK, and happy for us to take references and run a background check
Good enough English to understand job notes and chat with customers
Driving licence and your own car are a bonus (more jobs, more hours), not essential

How it works

1Apply — Fill in the 60-second form on this page — no CV needed.
2Quick chat — We call or message you within two working days for a friendly phone chat.
3Meet & trial — Meet the team in Sunbury-on-Thames, then do a paid trial clean alongside one of our experienced cleaners.
4Start — References and checks done, you get your first regular jobs and a rota that fits.
Prefer to talk first? Call 07942 369735, message us on WhatsApp or email contact@urbangleamcleaning.com with “Cleaner application” in the subject.
