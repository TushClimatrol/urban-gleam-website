How much does cleaning cost in London & Surrey? 2026 prices.

Cleaning prices in the UK are quoted three ways: by the hour, as a fixed price by property size, or per item. This guide explains what each costs around Sunbury-on-Thames, Surrey and South West London in 2026, what pushes a price up or down, and the questions worth asking before you book. Our own prices are shown alongside so you can compare.

By the Urban Gleam team · Updated 18 October 2026 · 4 min read

Hourly cleaning rates

Regular domestic cleaning is almost always priced per hour, per cleaner. Across Surrey and outer South West London, independent cleaners typically charge £15–£20 an hour (directory listings for Sunbury-on-Thames show £17–£23), while insured cleaning companies that supply products, equipment and a replacement if your cleaner is ill charge £20–£30 an hour. Inner London rates run a few pounds higher.

A useful rule of thumb for a regular clean is one hour per bedroom plus one hour: a two-bedroom flat takes about three hours, a four-bedroom house about five. The first visit usually runs longer because it is really a light deep clean.

End of tenancy cleaning prices

End of tenancy cleaning is normally a fixed price by property size, because the job is defined by the letting agent’s checklist rather than by time. Expect to pay roughly £120–£180 for a studio, £150–£220 for a one-bed, £200–£300 for a two-bed and £300–£450 for a three-bed house in Surrey and outer London, with professional carpet cleaning adding £40–£100 depending on the number of rooms.

Cleaning is consistently the most common reason tenancy deposits are disputed in the UK. A fixed-price professional clean with a receipt is the simplest way to protect a deposit that is usually five weeks’ rent. See our end of tenancy cleaning checklist.

After builders cleaning prices

After builders (post-renovation) cleaning is priced like end of tenancy cleaning — by property size — but quotes vary more because the amount of fine dust, paint and adhesive residue differs so much from job to job. In the UK, a full after builders clean typically costs £150–£500 for a house, or around £25–£40 an hour per cleaner where it is quoted hourly. Ask whether the price includes inside cupboards, window frames and light fittings, and whether a second “settle dust” visit a few days later is included or extra.

Our after builders prices are the same fixed prices as end of tenancy cleaning: from £130 for a studio. Full details on our after builders cleaning page.

Oven, carpet and window cleaning prices

Specialist cleans are priced per item rather than by time:

  • Oven cleaning: £45–£70 for a single oven, £65–£95 for a double, £85–£130 for a range cooker. Urban Gleam: single £45, double £65, range £85.
  • Carpet cleaning: £20–£40 per room with a minimum call-out of £50–£80; a three-piece suite £60–£120. Urban Gleam: £60 for one room, £75 for three, sofas from £40.
  • Window cleaning: £10–£20 for a flat or small terraced house outside, £20–£40 for a semi or detached house, with inside glass adding roughly 50%. Urban Gleam: from £15.

What changes the price

  • Condition. A home that is cleaned regularly costs less per visit than one that has not been cleaned for months; heavy limescale, grease or mould adds time.
  • Products and equipment. Some cleaners expect you to provide them; companies usually include them. Eco-friendly products no longer carry a premium with most firms, including ours.
  • Frequency. Weekly and fortnightly cleans are often cheaper per hour than one-offs, and many companies have a longer minimum for one-off and deep cleans.
  • Access and parking. Controlled parking zones in London boroughs and no on-site parking can add a parking charge; Spelthorne and most of Surrey rarely do.
  • Insurance and vetting. Public liability insurance, DBS checks and a replacement cleaner when yours is ill are what you pay for above the independent-cleaner rate.
  • Extras. Inside the oven, inside the fridge, interior windows, ironing and balconies are usually priced as add-ons of £15–£30 each.

How to compare quotes

  • Ask whether the price is per cleaner — two cleaners for two hours is four cleaning hours, not two.
  • Check that products and equipment are included, and that the vacuum has a HEPA filter if anyone in the house has allergies.
  • For end of tenancy, ask for a fixed price and a written list of what is included, and whether they will return free of charge if the agent raises an issue.
  • Ask about the minimum charge and whether there is a deposit. (We have no deposit — you pay after the clean.)
  • Look for recent Google reviews with photos; the best evidence of a good clean is a before-and-after picture.
